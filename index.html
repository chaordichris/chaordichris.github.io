About

A working index.

chaordichris is a place to keep ideas in view: complex systems, useful tools, good questions, and the occasional unfinished thought.

The name borrows from chaordic: the productive tension between chaos and order. It is a useful lens for work, learning, and the natural world.

Writing is kept brief. Projects appear when there is something real to show.

For ongoing fragments and code, find chaordichris on X and GitHub.
