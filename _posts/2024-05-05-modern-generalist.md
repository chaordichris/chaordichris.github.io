title: The modern generalist
description: Breadth is useful when it is paired with the patience to make something.
---

There has never been a better time to be broadly curious. The raw materials of learning are unusually accessible, and a small, specific idea can now travel a long way.

That access changes where the edge lives. Information is increasingly shared; judgment, practice, and follow-through remain scarce. The work is not simply to know a little about many things. It is to notice useful connections, then stay with one long enough to make it real.

Three habits seem durable:

* **Practice the craft.** A tool becomes useful only after repetition has made it instinctive.
* **Keep the range.** Apparently distant fields often share structure; a varied reference set makes those patterns easier to see.
* **Stay adaptable.** Small experiments reveal more than large declarations, especially in changing environments.

Breadth is not an alternative to depth. It is a way of choosing where depth may be most valuable.
